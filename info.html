Shipping

Orders ship from New York within 2 to 4 business days. You get a tracking number by email or text the moment the label prints.

  • US: $8 flat, free on orders over $150. Usually 2 to 5 days in transit.
  • International: calculated at checkout. Duties and taxes are the buyer's responsibility.
  • Drops can take longer. If a release is pre-order, the ship window is on the product page.

Placeholder terms. Confirm rates and windows before launch.

Returns & exchanges

Unworn, unwashed pieces with tags can be exchanged for a different size within 14 days of delivery, subject to stock. Limited drops and 1 of 1 pieces are final sale.

To start an exchange, message us with your order number and the size you need. Return shipping is on the customer; we cover shipping the replacement.

Something arrived wrong or damaged? Send a photo within 7 days and we make it right.
